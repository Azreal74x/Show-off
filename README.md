# **Thanks for checking out Monsterville!**

This is a school project where we worked together in a multidisciplinary team of four artists, two engineers, and one designer to bring elementary kids' creativity to life. We asked three kids to draw a monster and created a 3D game surrounding them!
#
**Meet our digital monsters Furbie, Jemie, and Tamara.**

Each monsters traits, rooms, and minigames are based on the kids' requests: Tamara loves gold coated candy and collects them in her minigame, Jemie's voice lines are recorded by its little artist and heard when you pet him, and Furbie loves soccer which you can tell by his room. These are only examples, discover more by playing the game!
